Who We Are

We are a Catholic fraternal organization rooted in faith, charity, and fellowship.

Our Mission

More than 2.2 million men in more than 17,000 councils around the world call themselves brothers in the Knights of Columbus. The Knights of Columbus empowers Catholic men to live their faith and serve their family, parish, community and country.

Our Vision

We offer formation, mutual aid and financial security to members through our charity, products and outreach. We aspire to invite every Catholic man to join the Knights of Columbus and to extend our charitable reach as widely as possible.

Our Values

Founded by Blessed Michael McGivney in 1882, the Knights of Columbus is the premier lay Catholic men's organization. Guided by the principles of charity, unity and fraternity, we build lifelong relationships with our members and families. We are multigenerational and international. We promote charity, unity, fraternity, and patriotism in everything we do for our church and community.
