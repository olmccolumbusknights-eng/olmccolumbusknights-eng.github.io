Welcome to Our Lady Of Mount Carmel

Knights of Columbus Council

Locally, our Council is a family oriented group dedicated to the principles of the Order. We are named after our parish, Our Lady of Mt. Carmel, to which we give our fullest support.

Founded by Blessed Fr. Michael J. McGivney in New Haven, Connecticut, in 1882, and named in honor of the explorer Christopher Columbus, the Knights of Columbus is the world's largest Catholic fraternal service organization. Originally serving as a mutual benefit society to working-class and immigrant Catholics in the United States, it developed into a fraternal benefit society dedicated to providing charitable services, including war and disaster relief, actively defending Catholicism in various nations, and promoting Catholic education.

The Knights also support the Catholic Church's positions on public policy issues, including various political causes, and are participants in the new evangelization.

As of 2020, there are over 2 million members around the world. Membership is limited to practicing Catholic men aged 18 or older. The order consists of four principles, Charity, Unity, Fraternity and Patriotism. There are over 17,000 local councils, including over 300 on college campuses, are chartered in the United States, Canada, Mexico, Philippines, Guam, South Korea and in Europe. The order's patriotic arm, the Fourth Degree, has more than 2,500 local assemblies.

Pope Saint John Paul II referred to the order as the "strong right arm of the Church" for their support of the church, as well as for their philanthropic and charitable efforts. In 2018, The Knights gave $185,682,989 directly to charity and performed over 75,640,244 man-hours of voluntary service.
